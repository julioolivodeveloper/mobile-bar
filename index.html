Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking your mobile bar.

Pricing depends on the number of guests, hours of service, and the cocktail menu selected. We offer custom quotes for every event — contact us by WhatsApp or phone and we'll send you a personalized estimate with no obligation.

Everything! We bring the full bar setup, ice, cups, straws, bar tools, fresh ingredients, and premium spirits. Carlos and Ara handle delivery, setup, service during your event, and complete cleanup afterward. You don't have to lift a finger.

We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance, especially for weekend events. Peak season (May through October) fills up fast, so the sooner the better. Check our availability calendar or message us to confirm your date.

We are based in Goshen, Indiana and serve the entire Michiana region including Elkhart, South Bend, Mishawaka, Nappanee, and surrounding areas. Not sure if we cover your location? Send us a message and we'll let you know!

Absolutely. Every cocktail on our menu has a mocktail version for guests who prefer non-alcoholic drinks. We also offer our popular Kids Lemonade station — perfect for quinceañeras, birthdays, and family events where all ages are present.

Yes! We have served events with over 200 guests and counting. Our setup is designed for high-volume service without sacrificing drink quality. For larger events we recommend contacting us early so we can plan the right staffing and supplies.

Yes — outdoor events are our specialty! Backyards, parks, patios, ranches, and open fields are all fair game. Our bar is self-contained and weather-ready. Just tell us the location and we'll take care of everything else.

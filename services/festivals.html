We go where the party is

Whether it's a neighborhood block party, a company picnic, an outdoor festival, or a large birthday bash, Lemon & Lime Bar Mobile has the capacity, the equipment, and the energy to serve your crowd efficiently and with style.

We specialize in high-volume service without sacrificing quality. Every cocktail is made fresh with real ingredients — no shortcuts, no shortcuts, just great drinks and fast service so your guests spend more time enjoying and less time waiting.

From Goshen and Elkhart to South Bend and across the Michiana region, we've served festivals and parties of all sizes. With over 200 events under our belt, we know how to handle a crowd.

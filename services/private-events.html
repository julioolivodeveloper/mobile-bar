Any space becomes the best bar

Not every great party happens in a venue. Some of the best celebrations are in backyards, parks, garages, and living rooms — and that's exactly where Lemon & Lime Bar Mobile thrives.

We bring everything: the bar, the ice, the cups, the tools, the cocktails, and the bartenders. You pick the place, we take care of the rest. No venue required. No stress for you.

Mojito vs Margarita: Which Cocktail
Should You Serve at Your Event?

June 10, 2025  ·  Lemon & Lime Bar Mobile

It's one of the most common questions we get when clients are planning their event menu: "Should we go with Mojitos or Margaritas?" Both are wildly popular, both are on our menu, and both will make your guests happy. But they are genuinely different drinks that serve different moments differently. Let's break it all the way down.

The Mojito: Refreshing, Elegant, Approachable

The Mojito is a Cuban-born cocktail built on a simple but beautiful combination: white rum, fresh lime juice, simple syrup, fresh mint leaves, and sparkling soda water. It's served tall over ice and finished with a mint sprig. The result is aromatic, bright, and unmistakably refreshing — the kind of drink that makes you feel like you're somewhere warm and wonderful.

Why the Mojito Works

The Mojito's biggest strength is its broad appeal. The mint makes it feel light and fresh, not heavy or boozy. The rum is present but not aggressive. Guests who don't usually drink cocktails will often reach for a Mojito because it doesn't feel intimidating. People who are pacing themselves through a long event love it because it feels refreshing rather than intoxicating. The tall glass and the fresh mint sprig also make it one of the most photogenic cocktails we serve — it looks stunning in photos.

Another practical advantage: a Virgin Mojito (without the rum) is one of the easiest and most satisfying mocktails to make. It's essentially sparkling mint lemonade, and non-drinking guests absolutely love it. One recipe serves two crowds.

The Margarita: Bold, Festive, Iconic

The Margarita is one of the most-ordered cocktails in the United States, and for good reason. Tequila, fresh lime juice, and triple sec or Cointreau — shaken and served on the rocks or blended, with a salted rim. It's bold, citrusy, and tequila-forward in a way that feels celebratory and fun. There's a reason people associate Margaritas with parties: the drink carries an energy to it that a Mojito, for all its elegance, simply doesn't.

Why the Margarita Works

The Margarita's salted rim is a sensory experience in itself — the contrast of salty and sweet and sour is deeply satisfying in a way that's hard to explain until you've had a great one. It pairs brilliantly with food (especially anything with heat or richness), which makes it an excellent choice for events that include a meal. Tequila fans are vocal and passionate, and a well-made Margarita will generate more compliments at the bar than almost any other drink we serve.

The Margarita is also endlessly customizable: spicy jalapeño, strawberry, mango, frozen blended, on the rocks, with mezcal — the variations are limitless and each one feels like a different cocktail. That versatility makes it a natural anchor for a signature event cocktail.

Which Wins for Specific Events?

Weddings

For formal or semi-formal weddings in Goshen, the Mojito often wins on elegance. It feels refined and is easy to serve quickly during cocktail hour. That said, many of our wedding clients request both — Mojitos during the cocktail hour when guests are standing and mingling, Margaritas once the dancing starts and the energy picks up. That's genuinely the ideal setup.

Quinceañeras

At quinceañeras, the Margarita tends to dominate. The festive, bold flavor profile matches the energy of the celebration, and tequila has deep cultural roots at these events. If you're going to pick one, go Margarita. But again — having both is never a wrong answer.

Summer Outdoor Parties

On a hot Indiana summer afternoon, the Mojito has a slight edge. The mint and soda water make it feel genuinely cooling — it's one of those rare drinks that actually helps you feel less hot while you're drinking it outdoors. The Margarita is wonderful in summer too, but it's richer and the tequila heat can feel slightly heavier on a 90-degree day.

Winter or Indoor Events

Flip it around: at an indoor winter event or a holiday party, the Margarita's boldness and warmth make it a better fit. The Mojito's refreshing lightness can feel slightly out of place when people are wearing sweaters and it's 20 degrees outside. A Margarita feels festive regardless of season; a Mojito feels most at home in warm weather.

The Real Answer: Serve Both

Here's the truth we tell every client who asks this question: you don't have to choose. Both the Mojito and the Margarita are on the Lemon & Lime Bar Mobile menu, and serving both gives your guests options that match their mood and their tolerance. The Mojito crowd and the Margarita crowd exist simultaneously at every event we've ever worked. Let them both be happy.

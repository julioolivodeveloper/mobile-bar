10 Best Cocktails for Weddings
in Goshen, Indiana

August 15, 2025  ·  Lemon & Lime Bar Mobile

Your wedding bar is one of the most talked-about details of the entire reception. Guests may forget the centerpieces, but they will absolutely remember whether the drinks were good — or whether they spent the night waiting in a long line for something mediocre. In Goshen, Indiana, where weddings range from elegant barn receptions to lively backyard celebrations, having the right cocktail menu makes all the difference.

At Lemon & Lime Bar Mobile, we've served countless weddings across the Michiana area. We've learned what people actually order, what keeps lines moving fast, and what cocktails generate the most compliments at the end of the night. Here are the 10 cocktails we recommend most for Indiana weddings.

The 10 Best Wedding Cocktails

1. Classic Margarita

The Margarita is the undisputed crowd-pleaser at nearly every wedding we've served. Fresh lime juice, tequila, and triple sec over ice with a salted rim — it's festive, refreshing, and instantly recognizable. Guests who don't have a strong cocktail preference will always say yes to a Margarita, making it a must-have anchor for your menu.

2. Paloma

The Paloma — tequila with fresh grapefruit juice and a splash of sparkling water — has surged in popularity over the past few years, and for good reason. It's slightly lighter than a Margarita, has a beautiful pink hue in the glass, and feels elevated without being intimidating. It's perfect for afternoon and outdoor weddings in the Indiana summer heat.

3. Mojito

Mint, lime, rum, sugar, and soda water — the Mojito is the definition of refreshing elegance. It looks stunning in a tall glass with a fresh mint sprig, photographs beautifully for those candid reception shots, and appeals to guests who prefer something lighter and more aromatic. A Mojito station at a wedding always draws a crowd.

4. Michelada

If your wedding guest list includes people who love Mexican-style drinks, a Michelada is a must. Beer mixed with lime juice, hot sauce, Worcestershire, and a Tajín rim — it's bold, savory, and uniquely satisfying. It's especially popular with guests who don't want a spirit-forward cocktail but still want something more exciting than a plain beer.

5. Vampiro

The Vampiro is a step up from the Michelada — tequila replaces the beer as the base, mixed with tomato juice, lime, and a spicy kick. It's richer and more complex, with a deep red color that makes it visually striking. Guests who try a Vampiro for the first time at a wedding almost always ask for a second one.

6. Calimocho

Equal parts red wine and cola over ice — the Calimocho is a Spanish-origin drink that has found a passionate following at Latino celebrations across Indiana. It sounds unconventional but tastes incredible: smooth, slightly sweet, and easy to drink all evening. It's also one of the most budget-friendly options on any wedding bar menu.

7. Mezcal Cocktail

For the guests who like something smoky and sophisticated, a Mezcal-based cocktail — whether a Mezcal Negroni, a smoky Paloma, or simply mezcal on the rocks with an orange slice — signals that your bar is the real deal. Mezcal drinkers are passionate, and they will remember and talk about your wedding bar for years.

8. Kids Lemonade

Not a cocktail, but absolutely essential. Weddings have guests of all ages, and children (plus pregnant guests and designated drivers) deserve something special too. Fresh lemonade served in mason jars with colorful straws and a citrus slice makes non-drinkers feel included and keeps the little ones happy so the adults can dance.

9. Champagne Toast (Add-On)

The champagne moment — when everyone raises a glass for the first toast — is one of the most iconic wedding traditions. We offer champagne toasts as an add-on service, making sure every single guest has a glass in hand exactly when you need it. It's one of those details that photographs perfectly and creates a memory that lasts a lifetime.

10. Signature Custom Cocktail

Our favorite offering for weddings is the custom signature cocktail — a drink named after the couple, built around their favorite flavors. Whether it's a spicy jalapeño Margarita named "The Reyes Sunset" or a tropical Mojito called "Carlos & Ana's Kiss," a signature cocktail adds a personal touch that makes your wedding bar completely one-of-a-kind.

One Key Tip: Book Early

Wedding season in Goshen runs hot from May through October, and Saturdays fill up months in advance. If you're planning a wedding, reach out to us as early as possible — ideally 2 to 3 months before your date. This gives us time to collaborate on your menu, plan quantities based on your guest count, and make sure everything runs perfectly on your big day.

The best wedding bars aren't just about the drinks — they're about the experience. Our team shows up early, sets up completely, serves with energy and professionalism, and cleans up everything so you don't have to think about it. Your job is to enjoy your wedding. Our job is to make sure your guests talk about the bar all night long.

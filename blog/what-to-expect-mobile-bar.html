What to Expect When You Book
a Mobile Bar in Indiana

May 20, 2025  ·  Lemon & Lime Bar Mobile

If you've never hired a mobile bar service before, it's completely normal to have questions. What exactly happens after you make that first call? What do you need to provide? When does the bar show up? What does "all-inclusive" actually mean in practice? This guide walks you through the entire process — from your very first contact with us to the moment we drive away after a successful event. No surprises, no mysteries. Just a clear picture of what great mobile bar service looks like.

Step 1: Initial Contact

Everything starts with a conversation. You can reach us by phone at (574) 202-5420 or (574) 238-1464, or message us directly on WhatsApp. When you first reach out, the most helpful things to have ready are:

  • Your event date
  • Your approximate guest count
  • The event location (city, indoor vs. outdoor, venue type)
  • The type of event (wedding, quinceañera, birthday, corporate, etc.)
  • Any specific drinks you definitely want on the menu

You don't need to have everything figured out at this point — that's what the rest of the process is for. Just enough information for us to understand what you're working with and whether your date is available.

Step 2: Custom Quote

Within 24 hours of your initial contact, we'll send you a personalized quote based on your event details. Our quotes are not one-size-fits-all templates — they're built around your specific guest count, event duration, location, and the drinks you want. We'll tell you exactly what's included, what the pricing looks like, and what options you have to customize. There are no hidden fees or surprises.

If you have questions about the quote — anything at all — just ask. We'd rather spend an extra few minutes clarifying than have you show up to your event with the wrong expectations.

Step 3: Menu Planning

Once you're ready to move forward, we collaborate on your cocktail menu. This is one of our favorite parts of the process. We'll talk through what your crowd typically enjoys, what the event vibe calls for, whether there are cultural traditions we should accommodate, and whether there are guests (kids, pregnant guests, non-drinkers) who need non-alcoholic options.

This is also when we can design a signature cocktail if you want one — a custom drink named after you, the couple, or the occasion. A signature cocktail is one of those personal touches that guests remember and talk about long after the event.

Step 4: Confirmation and Deposit

To secure your date, we'll confirm the booking and collect a deposit. This locks in your event date and ensures we can plan your quantities and staffing properly. We'll confirm all the final details — address, start time, parking/access notes, and any last-minute menu adjustments — in the days leading up to your event.

Step 5: Day of the Event — We Arrive Early

On the day of your event, our team arrives 45 to 60 minutes before the bar service is scheduled to begin. That's not when we walk in — that's when we start setup. We unload our equipment, arrange the bar, stock the coolers, prep the garnishes, and make sure everything is in place before a single guest walks up.

You won't need to direct us much at this stage — we know what we're doing. All we ask is that you've arranged access to the event space and that someone is available to point us toward our setup area. After that, we take it from there.

Step 6: Service — Energy, Skill, and Warmth

Once the bar is open, Carlos, Ara, and our team go to work. We serve every guest with care — making each drink to order, managing the line efficiently so no one waits too long, and engaging with guests in a way that adds to the energy of the event rather than just being a transactional counter. Our bartenders are experienced, professional, and genuinely enjoy what they do. That shows.

Throughout service, we monitor supplies proactively. If we're running low on ice or a particular mixer, we handle it ourselves without pulling the host away. You're at your event. We're at work. That's the deal.

Step 7: Cleanup — We Leave No Trace

When service ends, we break down the bar completely. All our equipment comes with us. We clean our setup area, remove all our waste (cups, bottles, garnish scraps), and leave the space the way we found it. You don't lift a finger. The only thing left behind is a memorable evening and very happy guests.

Many of our clients tell us after the event that cleanup being handled was the detail they appreciated most. After a long, emotional celebration, the last thing anyone wants to do is deal with bar breakdown. We take care of it — that's part of what you hired us for.

The Bottom Line

Booking a mobile bar in Indiana should be easy, clear, and reassuring — not stressful. Our job is to handle an important piece of your event so well that you don't have to think about it at all. From the first call to the last drink of the night, you're in good hands with Lemon & Lime Bar Mobile. Call us at (574) 202-5420 to get started.

Best Summer Party Drinks
for Events in Michiana, Indiana

May 5, 2025  ·  Lemon & Lime Bar Mobile

Indiana summers hit different. From late May through September, Michiana delivers long, warm evenings that are genuinely perfect for outdoor events — backyard parties, pool parties, open-air quinceañeras, summer weddings under the open sky. And when it's hot and the music is going and everyone is in a great mood, the right drink makes everything better. The wrong drink — something heavy, overly sweet, or served warm — makes people sluggish and thirsty.

Here at Lemon & Lime Bar Mobile, we've learned over years of outdoor events in Goshen and Elkhart exactly which cocktails perform best in summer heat. These are the seven drinks your guests will be reaching for all evening.

The 7 Best Summer Party Drinks in Michiana

1. Mojito — The Summer Classic

If there is a single cocktail built for summer, it is the Mojito. Fresh mint, lime juice, white rum, simple syrup, and sparkling soda water — every element of this drink is designed to make you feel cooler. The mint creates a genuine cooling sensation; the soda water adds effervescence; the citrus keeps everything bright and lively. Served tall over ice with a fresh mint sprig, a Mojito is both deeply refreshing and visually striking. At outdoor summer events, it consistently ranks as our most-ordered cocktail.

2. Paloma — Grapefruit Is the Ultimate Summer Fruit

The Paloma — tequila, fresh grapefruit juice, lime, and a splash of sparkling water — is perhaps the most underrated summer cocktail on the planet. Grapefruit has a natural bittersweet brightness that feels tailor-made for warm weather. It's less sweet than a Margarita, slightly more complex, and has a beautiful blush-pink color that looks stunning in a glass. Many guests who try a Paloma for the first time in summer immediately make it their new warm-weather go-to drink.

3. Classic Margarita — Salty, Cold, and Perfect on a Hot Day

The Margarita needs no introduction, but it deserves special mention in a summer context. There is something almost physiologically satisfying about a salty Margarita rim on a hot afternoon — the salt helps your body retain hydration while the cold cocktail brings your temperature down. A Margarita served on the rocks with fresh lime juice (not pre-mix) on a 90-degree Indiana day is a near-perfect sensory experience. It's bold enough to feel celebratory and refreshing enough to drink outdoors without feeling heavy.

4. Michelada — The Beer-Based Solution for Long Outdoor Events

At events that run four, five, or six hours in the summer heat, the Michelada is your guests' best friend. Because it's beer-based — a light Mexican lager mixed with lime, hot sauce, Worcestershire, and Tajín — the alcohol content is relatively low and the drink is hydrating and savory rather than sweet and boozy. It's the kind of drink people can have two or three of over a long afternoon without losing their feet. It also pairs brilliantly with food, making it a natural choice for events that include a taco bar or outdoor grill.

5. Kids Lemonade — Essential for Family Outdoor Events

At any outdoor family event in Indiana, there will be children — and those kids deserve something just as special as what the adults are drinking. Fresh-squeezed lemonade served cold with a colorful straw and a citrus garnish is genuinely one of the most satisfying summer drinks at any age. Parents appreciate having a dedicated kids option rather than just water, and the lemonade station becomes a gathering point that kids remember fondly. We treat our lemonade with the same care as our cocktails — fresh, cold, and properly garnished.

6. Calimocho — The Unexpected Summer Hit

The Calimocho — red wine and cola over ice — surprises people at outdoor events every single time. It sounds unconventional, but the combination is genuinely delicious: the cola adds sweetness and carbonation that lightens the wine, and the result is surprisingly refreshing on a hot day. The Spanish have been drinking it at outdoor summer festivals for decades. At Michiana summer events, it has quietly become one of our most-talked-about items — guests who try it on a whim end up ordering a second one within the hour.

7. Virgin Mojito and Mocktails — For Everyone

A truly great summer bar serves everyone, not just the people drinking alcohol. A Virgin Mojito — fresh mint, lime, simple syrup, and sparkling water — is one of the most satisfying non-alcoholic drinks imaginable on a hot day. It looks identical to its alcoholic counterpart and tastes every bit as refreshing. For guests who are pregnant, designated driving, or simply not drinking, a thoughtfully made mocktail is a gesture that doesn't go unnoticed. We include non-alcoholic options with every booking.

A Note About Indiana Summer Weather and Outdoor Setups

Indiana summers can swing between beautiful dry warmth and humid heat with afternoon storms. When you're planning an outdoor bar setup in Michiana, always have a shade plan for the bar station (we bring our own setup, but placement matters), make sure there's access to electricity if you want blended frozen cocktails, and communicate with your venue or property about the hottest part of the afternoon if you're planning a midday event.

The good news: Goshen and Elkhart evenings in summer are typically stunning — warm but comfortable, with long golden light that makes outdoor events feel magical. It's genuinely some of the best outdoor entertaining weather in the Midwest, and the right cocktail menu makes the most of it.

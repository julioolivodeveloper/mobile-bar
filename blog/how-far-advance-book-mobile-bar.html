How Far in Advance Should You
Book a Mobile Bar in Indiana?

April 5, 2025  ·  Lemon & Lime Bar Mobile

This is one of the most common planning questions we receive at Lemon & Lime Bar Mobile — and the honest answer is: it depends on your event type, your season, and your day of the week. The general rule of thumb is 2 to 4 weeks minimum, but there are situations where you should book much earlier — and situations where we can work with significantly less notice. Here's a complete breakdown to help you plan properly.

The General Rule: 2 to 4 Weeks Minimum

For a typical event — a backyard birthday party, a small family gathering, a casual outdoor event on a weekday or non-peak weekend — reaching out 2 to 4 weeks before your event date is usually sufficient. This gives us enough time to confirm availability, send and discuss your quote, finalize the menu, and make sure we have everything ordered and prepped correctly for your specific event.

Booking with less than 2 weeks notice isn't impossible, but the options narrow quickly, especially as we get further into event season. Two weeks should be treated as a floor, not a comfortable cushion.

Peak Season (May–October): Book 4 to 8 Weeks Out or More

Indiana event season runs from late spring through early fall, and this is when demand for mobile bar services in Michiana is at its highest. Weddings, quinceañeras, graduation parties, family reunions, outdoor festivals — they all cluster in these months because the weather cooperates and people want to be outside.

During peak season, 4 to 8 weeks advance notice is much more realistic for securing your preferred date. Some of our most popular weekends in June, July, and August fill up even earlier than that. If you're planning a major summer event and have a specific date in mind, don't wait. Reach out to us as soon as you know — even if other details of your event aren't fully figured out yet. Locking in the date is the priority.

Saturdays Fill Fastest — Here's Why

Saturday is by far the most-requested day for events across Goshen, Elkhart, and all of Michiana. It's the day when people can celebrate without worrying about work the next morning, venues are most available, and guests can travel from further away. The result: Saturday slots during peak season are genuinely scarce, and they go fast.

If your event is on a Saturday between May and October, we strongly recommend reaching out 6 to 8 weeks in advance as a baseline — and sooner if your event is large, complex, or has a specific date that can't be moved (like a birthday that falls on a Saturday). Waiting until 3 weeks out for a peak-season Saturday is a gamble that doesn't always pay off.

Holidays and Holiday Weekends: Book 2 to 3 Months Out

Memorial Day weekend, the Fourth of July, Labor Day weekend, Cinco de Mayo — these are the highest-demand dates of the year for event services of all kinds. If your event falls on or near a major holiday, treat your booking timeline with the same urgency you'd use for a peak-season Saturday and then add some.

We recommend reaching out 2 to 3 months in advance for holiday weekends, full stop. These dates fill first, they fill fastest, and there's no way to recover if you miss the window. The earlier you lock in, the better your planning experience will be — we'll have more time to collaborate on your menu, work through any logistics questions, and make your event as good as it can be.

What Happens If You Wait Too Long

The most common consequence of waiting too long to book is simply that your preferred date is no longer available. A mobile bar service, unlike some vendors, can only serve one event at a time — and once a date is booked, it's gone. If you come to us two weeks before a peak-season Saturday and that date is already filled, there's nothing we can do except hope for a cancellation.

Beyond date availability, booking late also means less time for menu planning. A custom signature cocktail, a carefully calibrated quantity estimate, special accommodations for your guest mix — all of these take conversation and collaboration. When you book early, that planning time exists. When you book last-minute, you get whatever is possible in a compressed timeframe. Early booking isn't just about availability; it's about quality.

What About Last-Minute Bookings?

We won't pretend last-minute bookings never happen — they do, and we accommodate them when we can. Life is unpredictable. Sometimes an event gets planned on short notice. If you reach out less than 2 weeks before your event, contact us immediately and we'll check our calendar. If the date is available, we will absolutely work to make it happen.

The key is to contact us as soon as you know you need bar service — not after you've confirmed everything else. The bar is a central part of your event experience, and it deserves to be planned early rather than treated as an afterthought you figure out in the last week.
